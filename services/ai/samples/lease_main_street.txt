COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
(fictional sample for the Vero demo; not a real contract)

between

Main Street Real Estate GmbH, Hauptstraße 12, 60311 Frankfurt am Main
("Landlord")

and

Pizzeria Amalfi, owner Vincenzo Amalfi, Hauptstraße 14, 60311 Frankfurt am Main
("Tenant")

§ 1 Premises
The Landlord lets to the Tenant the ground-floor restaurant premises at Hauptstraße 14 (approx. 140 m²),
including the kitchen, the storage room in the basement and two parking spaces in the rear yard.

§ 2 Term
The lease starts on 1 January 2024 and runs for an indefinite period. Either party may terminate it with
six months' written notice to the end of a calendar quarter, at the earliest to 31 December 2028.

§ 3 Rent
(1) The monthly rent is EUR 3,500.00 (three thousand five hundred euros), payable in advance no later than
the third working day of each month to the Landlord's account.
(2) Operating costs (heating, water, waste) are included in the rent. Electricity is contracted by the
Tenant directly with the utility provider.

§ 4 Rent adjustment
The rent may be adjusted no more than once per calendar year in line with the consumer price index.
An adjustment requires a written notice from the Landlord stating the new rent and takes effect at the
earliest three months after the notice has been received.

§ 5 Deposit
The Tenant has provided a deposit of three monthly rents.

§ 6 Use
The premises may only be used to operate a restaurant. Any change of use requires the Landlord's prior
written consent.

§ 7 Final provisions
Changes to this agreement must be made in writing. German law applies.

Frankfurt am Main, 15 December 2023

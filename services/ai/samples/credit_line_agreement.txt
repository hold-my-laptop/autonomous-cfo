OVERDRAFT FACILITY AGREEMENT
(fictional sample for the Vero demo; Example Bank AG is not a real bank and this is not an offer)

Borrower: Pizzeria Amalfi, Vincenzo Amalfi, Hauptstraße 14, 60311 Frankfurt am Main
Lender: Example Bank AG, Frankfurt am Main

1. Facility
The Lender grants the Borrower a current-account overdraft facility of up to EUR 30,000.00 on account
DE00 0000 0000 0000 0000 00. The facility may be drawn and repaid at any time.

2. Interest
Interest on the amount actually drawn is charged at a fixed rate of 9.75 % per annum, calculated on the
basis of actual days elapsed and a 365-day year, and debited quarterly in arrears.
Amounts drawn above the agreed limit bear interest at 14.50 % per annum.

3. Fees
No arrangement fee and no fee on the undrawn amount are charged.

4. Term
The facility is available from the date of signature until 31 December 2025 and may be renewed.

5. Termination
The Lender may terminate the facility for good cause, in particular in case of a material deterioration
of the Borrower's financial situation.
